Strengths

I specialise in three things:

End-to-end product design: I take products from first research through to ready-to-build specs — discovery, flows, UI, and a handoff engineers can actually build from.

Systems thinking: I design reusable components and patterns instead of one-off screens, so products stay consistent and scale without breaking.

Designing under constraint: Tight timelines, small teams, and technical limits — I make pragmatic calls that ship and still solve the real problem.

How I Work

Design is not a straight line. It's a cycle. Every answer is just a better question.

01Define

Clarify the problem space, set success metrics, and align on scope before a single pixel gets designed.

02Research

Desk research, user interviews, and field observation to answer the right questions.

03Design

Sketches to wireframes to high-fidelity. Fast iteration, tight execution.

04Validate

Usability testing, real feedback, honest iteration. Ship what works, cut what doesn't.

Call Me Pari

I moved to the Netherlands because of the way people here think. Not just about design, about problems. The instinct to improve things together, incrementally, without ego. That's the culture I want to work in.

Professionally, I think in systems. It's not something I learned, it's just how my brain works. Conflicts surface early for me. Gaps between user journeys, broken handoffs, trust issues buried three flows deep, I catch them before they ship. Five years of being the only designer in the room will sharpen that instinct fast, and the most complex thing I've built, a full ERP for the oil and gas industry, taught me that holding the whole picture in your head is a skill not everyone has. I do, and I don't take it for granted.

I'm also just genuinely curious about people. High empathy isn't a soft skill for me — it's how I move through a user journey and feel where it breaks. I'm looking for a team building something complex, in a place where design actually matters.
